# ReplyPilot build notes

Built 2026-11-06. Working Manifest V3 Chrome extension, fully offline demo.

## What was built

- `manifest.json` — Manifest V3, action popup + side panel + options page, permissions limited to `storage` and `sidePanel`.
- `template-engine.js` — offline template engine and the single place the grounding rule is enforced. Extracts order number, tracking number and customer name from the customer's email, quotes policy values verbatim, and turns any missing fact into a `[NEEDS INFO: ...]` placeholder listed in the result.
- `llm-adapter.js` — separated stub for a future optional LLM pass. `generateWithLLM()` reports unavailable, so callers fall back to the template engine. No API key exists anywhere in this build.
- `policy.json` + `policy-store.js` — the editable store policy (fictional store Willow & Wick Home Goods). Load order: options-page edits in chrome.storage, then bundled policy.json, then an identical snapshot fallback for file:// demo use.
- `options.html` / `options.js` — options page with one field per policy line, save/reset, and JSON import/export.
- `sidepanel.html` / `popup.html` / `draft-app.js` — drafting surfaces. Paste an email or load a bundled sample, pick an intent (shipping, return, refund, damaged, general), generate, copy. An email staged by the demo inbox via chrome.storage is picked up automatically.
- `demo/inbox.html` — mock inbox for the fictional store, 5 sample customer emails, no Gmail access needed. Its button loads the selected email into the drafting flow on the page and stages it for the extension side panel when storage is available.
- `samples.js` — the 5 samples shared by the demo and the verification script.
- `test/verify.js` — Node verification, see below.

### Showcase draft, generated by the verification script

Sample 1: Maya Chen asks where order #1042 is. No tracking number appears in her email or in the policy file. Output draft, verbatim:

```text
Hi Maya,

Thanks for getting in touch, and I am sorry for the wait on order #1042.

I do not want to guess at your tracking details, so I have left this open for our team to fill in before sending: [NEEDS INFO: tracking number and current carrier status].

For reference: Orders are packed and handed to the carrier within 2 business days of purchase. Delivery is 3 to 5 business days within the United States, and 7 to 14 business days outside the United States. A tracking link is emailed as soon as the carrier scans the parcel.

If anything above does not match what you are seeing, reply to this email and we will check again.

Best regards,
The Willow & Wick support team
Willow & Wick Home Goods
```

The tracking gap is flagged, not filled. The verification script also asserts the draft contains no UPS-style or labelled tracking number pattern.

Contrast case, sample 5: Alex Kim's email itself provides tracking number 1Z999AA10123456784 for order #1055. That draft quotes that exact number back and contains no tracking placeholder, which the script asserts as well. Facts the customer supplied are usable; facts nobody supplied are never created.

## Known limits of this demo build

- Intent is picked by the seller, not auto-classified.
- Fact extraction is regex based and deliberately conservative: a tracking number is only recognised when the email labels it or it matches a UPS 1Z format.
- Policies are stored per browser profile (chrome.storage) or in the bundled policy.json. There is no sync, billing, or store-platform integration in this build.
- The extension never sends email. The seller copies the draft into their own mail tool and sends it themselves.
